What is a Training Step?

One action taken by the agent across all 16 parallel game instances. 50,000 steps ≈ 3,000 full games of experience. There are 5 million steps total across 4 curriculum stages, with an evaluation checkpoint every 50,000 steps — each dot on the Win Rates charts is one checkpoint.

What is a Turn?

One player's full move: roll dice → collect resources → optionally build, trade, or play a dev card → end turn. With 4 players, a round is 4 turns. A typical game runs 150–280 turns. The simulator caps at 300 turns, after which the game is scored by VP — shorter average game length means the agent is winning outright, not grinding to the cap.

Opponent Bots — ordered easiest → hardest
Random Agent
Picks uniformly at random from all legal actions. No strategy. The sanity-check floor — the agent should dominate it by end of Stage 1. Expected win rate in a 4-player game: ~25%.
Greedy Bot
Always takes the highest immediate VP gain: cities first, then settlements, then roads. Effective early but collapses late-game because it ignores dev cards and port efficiency.
SmartBot
Adapts strategy by VP: rushes settlements early, pivots to cities and dev cards mid-game, plays knights for Largest Army. Extends roads toward high-production hexes. ~Competent human level.
HybridBot
The strongest baseline. Combines aggressive early building with heavy dev-card investment — buys knights constantly, pursues Largest Army, pivots between roads and cities based on board state.
LongestRoad Bot
Single-mindedly builds roads for the Longest Road bonus (2 VP). Good at blocking opponents but weak if the bonus is stolen or opponents pivot to cities.
DevCard Bot
Hoards ore and wheat to buy dev cards as fast as possible, targeting Largest Army. Spikes hard mid-game but is vulnerable if ore/wheat production is denied.
Curriculum Stages
Stage 1 — vs Random (1M steps): Agent learns the rules. Win rate vs Random climbs from ~10% to ~90%.
Stage 2 — vs Mixed (1M steps): Opponents rotate across all four bots. Agent learns generalizable strategy instead of exploiting one opponent's weakness.
Stage 3 — vs Hybrid+Smart (1.5M steps): Focused grind against the two hardest baselines. Forces a coherent long-term strategy.
Stage 4 — Self-play (1.5M steps): Agent plays against past versions of itself plus HybridBot and SmartBot. Discovers strategies heuristic opponents never expose.
Key Metrics

Win Rate

Fraction of games won across 50 test games at each checkpoint. 25% = random chance in 4 players. Above that = learned strategy.

Avg Victory Points

Mean VP at game end. 10 VP wins. Higher VP even in losses means the agent is building effectively — more robust signal than win rate alone early in training.

Policy Loss

How much action probabilities changed this update. Decreases as the policy stabilises. Spikes are normal when the agent discovers new strategies.

Entropy

Reported on a negative scale — near 0 = very random, more negative = more decisive. Falls naturally as the agent converges on good strategies.

KL Divergence

Distance between old and new policy per update. PPO clips large jumps to ~0.015. Occasional spikes are fine; sustained high values mean learning rate is too aggressive.

Clip Fraction

Percentage of gradient updates clipped by PPO. Healthy range: 5–20%. Too high = policy changing too fast. Too low = very conservative, slow learning.
